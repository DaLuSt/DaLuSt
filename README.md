## 🙋 About Me

I am a **Data Professional** focused on making data understandable, governable, manageable and usable across an organisation.

My work sits at the intersection of **Data Architecture, Data Governance, Data Management and Digital Transformation** — connecting business needs, information, data, technology and organisational change.

* 🏗️ **Data Architect** — designing structures that connect business, information and technology
* 🏛️ **Data Governance** — establishing ownership, decision-making, standards, policies and accountability around data
* 🗂️ **Data Management** — improving how organisations create, manage, integrate, govern and use data
* 🧩 **Information & Semantic Architecture** — connecting business meaning to information models and technical implementations
* 🔄 **Digital Change Management** — helping organisations adopt new ways of working with data and technology
* 📊 **Data Science** — using analytical and scientific techniques to turn data into insight
* 🧬 Background in **Biomedical, Biotechnological and Health Sciences**
* 🚗 Currently working in the **Mobility sector within Government**

I am particularly interested in the question:

> **How do we turn organisational data from something we have to manage into something we can deliberately design?**

I see data management as a chain of connected capabilities:

```text
Business Strategy
       ↓
Business Capabilities & Processes
       ↓
Information & Semantics
       ↓
Data Architecture
       ↓
Data Governance & Management
       ↓
Data Platforms & Technology
       ↓
Data Products & Services
       ↓
Decision Making & Value
```

The interesting part is not any individual layer.

**The interesting part is how they fit together.**
